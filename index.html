02 — About the Engineer

I don't make websites. I design entire systems.

I'm Shivaprasad V — an Electronics & Communication engineer and the person behind SpDly Studios. I work at the seam where hardware meets signal meets software, and I am happiest when a problem has no obvious shape yet.

My practice is systems thinking: a project might begin as a question about radio noise, become a PCB, grow into a real-time pipeline, and end as a product someone can hold. Embedded firmware, communication systems, signal processing, robotics and full-stack software are simply the materials I reach for.

“Every project starts with a question. The engineering is just the discipline of answering it honestly.”
BasedChennai, Tamil Nadu, India
StudyingB.E. ECE — Madras Institute of Technology, Anna University

03 — Selected Work

Case
Studies

Proof, not promises. A few systems taken from question to working artefact.

  1. 01

    Wireless Occupancy Estimation

    Reading a room from ambient radio. A statistical-filtering pipeline that estimates occupancy from RSSI — no extra sensors, 89.47% accuracy.

    Comms · DSP2025
  2. 02

    GPS-Independent Localization

    Position from signal geometry alone. Triangulating a moving node using distributed receivers and real-time RSSI — infrastructure not required.

    Comms · Robotics2025
  3. 03

    Telemetry Ground Station

    Bridging the bench and the sky. An end-to-end link — sensors, embedded transport, decoding and a live dashboard — built from concept to hardware.

    Embedded · Comms2024
  4. 04

    Edge Signal Processor

    FIR design and statistical filtering running close to the metal — turning noisy analogue signals into decisions on constrained microcontrollers.

    DSP · Embedded2024
  5. 05

    Autonomous Rover Platform

    A small vehicle with big opinions. Cascaded PID, a complementary filter and sensor fusion keep it upright, aimed and moving on its own.

    Robotics · AI2025
